摘要 用于富含接触操作的动作条件世界模型需要捕获接触在不同力下如何形成、滑动和释放的数据。通过机器人远程操作收集此类数据的速度很慢，而且通常缺乏力反馈并且受到夹具几何形状的限制。我们询问这些动态是否可以从人类的身体互动中学习并转移到机器人上。我们提出了 ACTS，这是一种仅根据人类交互数据进行训练的视觉触觉世界模型。操作员操纵手持式 GelSight 传感器，同时我们记录第三人称和手腕视频、触觉图像、6 自由度传感器运动和触觉衍生法向力

English abstract

Abstract Action-conditioned world models for contact-rich manipulation require data that captures how contact forms, slides, and releases under varying force. Such data are slow to collect through robot teleoperation, which often lacks force feedback and is constrained by gripper geometry. We ask whether these dynamics can instead be learned from human physical interaction and transferred to a robot. We present ACTS, a visuo-tactile world model trained only on human interaction data. Operators manipulate handheld GelSight sensors while we record third-person and wrist video, tactile images, 6-DoF sensor motion, and tactile-derived normal force, yielding React, a 4.9-hour bimanual dataset across three objects.
