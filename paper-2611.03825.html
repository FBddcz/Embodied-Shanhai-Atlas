壹摘要

在机器人技术中，形状控制或形状伺服是指自动操纵可变形物体达到所需形状。本文是关于数据驱动、基于雅可比行列式的形状控制的两部分教程的第二部分，其中变形雅可比行列式是机器人运动如何改变测量形状的局部模型，是根据测量值估计的。第一部分构建了形状伺服环路并导出了局部稳定条件。本文首先使用变形雅可比行列式的奇异值分解来识别小型机器人运动产生的形状变化、需要大型机器人运动的形状变化以及那些需要大机器人运动的形状变化。

English abstract

In robotics, shape control, or shape servoing, refers to the automatic manipulation of a deformable object toward a desired shape. This article is the second of a two-part tutorial on data-driven, Jacobian-based shape control, in which the deformation Jacobian, a local model of how robot motion changes the measured shape, is estimated from measurements. Part I built the shape-servoing loop and derived a local stability condition. This paper starts by using the singular value decomposition of the deformation Jacobian to identify shape changes that can be produced by small robot motions, those that require large robot motions, and those that are locally unavailable. It then analyses how the choice of shape features determines how the controller regulates the object's shape and, therefore, how much the object deforms on its way to the target.
